Surviving H.L.L. (Novelization)

In the dying lands of Verenthia, the link with the Gods has dissolved, giving way to the perpetual gloom caused by the Great Storm. This is not a story of heroes, but of survivors: a broken tradesman haunted by his past, a scholar searching for a forbidden cure, and a cult seeking the unknown.

As they traverse a landscape twisted by cosmic horrors and relentless psychological trauma, they must confront the terrifying truth: the only way to escape is to surrender everything that makes them human. Madness is a currency, and salvation is a myth.
